## 课程结构

**结构说明**：至少 24 units（8 门研究生课程，每门 3 units）。IEMS 课程 15–24 units；Department of Information Engineering 的 ENGG / IERG 课程 0–6 units；学生可在 Option (a) 其他工程学院硕士课程（BMEG、CMSC、ECLT、ELEG、FTEC、MAEG、SEEM 5000-level，最多 6 units）或 Option (b) MSc in Business Analytics 的 DOTE 5000-level（最多 3 units）中二选一。另可修 IEMS5910 / IEMS5920 Advanced Research and Development Project I / II 及 IEMS5930 Information Engineering Internship。所有课程以英语授课，于沙田主校区面授；Academic 页课程清单标注「applicable to the 2026-27 academic year」，并注明并非每年所有课程均会开设、须视座位供应。

## 项目特色与就业

**项目特色**

- 官网：Established in 1997, the Master of Science in Information Engineering (MScIE) programme is a well-established taught postgraduate programme offered by the Department of Information Engineering at CUHK.
- 官网部门使命：Building a Connected, Secure and Smart Society in the Age of Digital Transformation
- 官网：Founded in 1989 by Professor Charles Kao, the father of optical communications, the Department of Information Engineering is the first and still the only academic department of its kind in Hong Kong.
- 官网研究方向：AI, Multimodal and Embodied Intelligence；Cyber-Security and Applied Cryptography；Information and Coding Theory；AI and Networked Systems；Optical Communications and Networking；Wireless Communications and Networked Systems；并涵盖 data science、big data analytics、blockchain、cloud computing、IoT。
- 官网 Programme Objectives：To excel in engineering and professional positions in industries and organizations that design, develop, deploy, or employ information systems, networks, and services；To develop a global perspective ... and be able to contribute to or lead interdisciplinary engineering projects；To continue their personal development through professional studies and life-long learning。
- 官网：flexible curriculum、holistic learning experience、industry exposure、extensive alumni network；可跨 Faculty of Engineering 与 Faculty of Business Administration 选课；提供 Advanced Research and Development Project I & II 与 Information Engineering Internship。

## 文书参考要点（PS / SOP）

- 呼应官网部门使命「Building a Connected, Secure and Smart Society in the Age of Digital Transformation」，说明你关注的信息工程议题（AI、cyber-security、networked systems 等）与部门研究强项的对应。
- 呼应官网 Programme Objectives「be able to contribute to or lead interdisciplinary engineering projects」与「develop a global perspective」，用跨团队协作项目体现全球视野与跨领域领导潜力。
- 呼应官网「flexible curriculum」与跨院选课（其他工程学院硕士最多 6 units，或 MSc in Business Analytics 的 DOTE 5000-level 最多 3 units），说明拟如何组合技术与管理课程，并表明是否选择 IEMS5910/5920 研究项目或 IEMS5930 实习。
- 呼应官网第 ③ 条入学路径「having work experiences in Information Technology related industries」：非理工背景者用 IE 相关修课或 IT 行业经历论证 relevant background。
