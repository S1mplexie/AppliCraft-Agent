## 课程结构

**结构说明**：共 30 credits = 5 门核心（15 credits）+ 5 门选修（15 credits），每门 3 credits。全日制 1 年分 3 个 trimesters 及暑期；Core and elective courses are subject to change；Courses are taught in English, except when Chinese is preferable；部分选修或以隔年密集课程 / 暑期形式开设。

**核心课程 / 必修**

- Statistical Analysis
- Decision Models and Applications
- Data Mining for Managers
- Machine Learning for Business I
- Advanced Business Analytics Practicum (DOTE6696BA)

**选修课程 / 方向**

- Economic Analytics
- Managing Service Operations
- Web Analytics and Intelligence
- Business Process Analysis and Simulation
- Fintech and Its Applications
- Database and Big Data Management
- Technology Field Study
- Supply Chain and Logistics Management
- Risk and Operations Analytics
- Generative Artificial Intelligence for Business
- Machine Learning for Business II
- Business Field Study

**Track / 专攻方向**

官网未分设 track

**项目特色**

- 官网（商学院硕士首页）：This data-driven programme hones participants' ability to extract meaningful information and insights from mass business data and make effective business decisions.
- 官网（curriculum 页）：convert large amounts of data into meaningful information by asking the right questions, choosing the right technology and implementing the right algorithms
- 官网（简章）：Integration of business and data analysis study；The curriculum often incorporates the latest trends in analytics, such as machine learning and artificial intelligence
- 官网（简章）：Advanced Business Analytics Practicum (DOTE6696BA) bridges the gap between classroom and real world —— 与企业 / 机构合作完成真实商业分析项目，由课程教师、faculty mentor 与赞助企业主管联合指导。
- 官网（商学院新闻稿）：2024 年 4 月庆祝 MSc in Business Analytics 项目 10 周年；校友论坛分享涵盖 internet technology、banking、finance、consulting 等行业。
- 官网（2026-2027 简章班级画像）：平均年龄 23、平均 GMAT 681、平均 13 个月工作经验；本科背景 Business & Economics 37.9%、Finance 20.9%、Accounting 11.8%、IT 5.9%、Math & Statistics 4.6%、Engineering 3.3%、Science 3.3%、Others 12.4%。

## 文书参考要点（PS / SOP）

- 呼应官网 curriculum 原文「asking the right questions, choosing the right technology and implementing the right algorithms」，用一段商业问题说明你如何定义问题、选择工具并落地算法。
- 呼应官网「Integration of business and data analysis study」与「extract meaningful information and insights from mass business data and make effective business decisions」，突出数据到商业决策的转化，而非纯技术堆叠。
- 呼应官网必修课 Advanced Business Analytics Practicum（DOTE6696BA）「bridges the gap between classroom and real world」，说明希望参与企业真实项目、团队协作与跨职能沟通的经历。
- 呼应官网「GMAT / GRE scores and work experience are not mandatory, but a good GMAT / GRE score or proof(s) of work experience will strengthen the application」，用量化项目 / 实习 / 工作经历补强文书，并可从 Generative Artificial Intelligence for Business、Fintech and Its Applications 等官方选修中点名具体方向。
