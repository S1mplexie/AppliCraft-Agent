## 课程结构

**结构说明**：至少 8 门课 / 24 units = 9 units 必修 + 15 units 选修；选修可由 courses、internship 或 industrial project 构成。选修分三类：Technology-Oriented Electives、Business/Application-Oriented Electives、Enrichment Training。三条 track 依兴趣与职业取向选择：Course Track（3 必修 + 5 选修）、Practicum Track（3 必修 + 4 选修 + 3 units 实习）、Industrial Project Track（3 必修 + 3 选修 + 6 units 两学期 industrial project）。具金融背景者可豁免必修 FINA6122 Financial Markets and Instruments，须另补一门选修。FTEC5540 实习须至少 12 continuous weeks 并参加监管机构工作坊；FTEC5910 学分须与后续学期修读 FTEC5920 一并确认。Individual courses and the programme curriculum are subject to review and revision without prior notice.

**核心课程 / 必修**

- FINA6122 Financial Markets and Instruments
- FTEC5510 Advanced Financial Infrastructure
- FTEC5520 Applied Blockchain and Cryptocurrencies

## 项目特色与就业

**项目特色**

- 官网：FinTech is an emerging discipline that focuses on employing technological innovations in financial practices. It leverages on the cutting-edge developments of artificial intelligence, data sciences and information technology to revolutionize the financial industry.
- 官网：FinTech 学生将掌握 cyber security、blockchain、data science、AI 等技术用于 finance 与 e-commerce，同时具备 finance 与 law（financial markets、financial engineering、regulatory policy、contracts、intellectual property）广度理解；能 identify, formulate, and apply 知识解决问题。
- 重要纠偏：项目属 Faculty of Engineering / Division of Engineering，并非商学院项目；同院另设 PgD in Financial Technology（并行项目，非合并）。
- 官网（校方新闻稿）：2019/20 学年开办，首届收生约 50 人；强调 hands-on experience and industrial training，与本地金融监管机构合作提供培训及实习，安排实地参观 FinTech 公司；提供三种学习模式（课堂 / 实习 / 论文）。
- 官网：Three tracks are available for students to choose depending on their interest and career aspirations；实习通常于完成前两学期后的暑期进行，至少 12 continuous weeks，并参加 HKMA、SFC、Insurance Authority 等监管机构工作坊。
- 官网（Admission 页）：Our graduates are expected to have acquired comprehensive, in-depth, state-of-the-art knowledge and relevant updated expert skills in FinTech and related technologies ... accumulated some experience in practical training, project development, or research activities.

**就业去向（仅官网明确表述）**

- 官网校方新闻稿明确：毕业生适合 banking、insurance、asset management、internet finance、FinTech start-ups、government regulatory agencies 等技术性岗位
- 系统性就业率 / 雇主统计：待核实

## 文书参考要点（PS / SOP）

- 呼应官网「good knowledge of mathematics (such as calculus, linear algebra and probability) and proficiency in at least one modern programming language (such as C, C++, Java or Python) as shown in academic transcript(s)」，用成绩单与项目直接回应数学与编程偏好。
- 呼应官网「Three tracks are available for students to choose depending on their interest and career aspirations」，说明选择 Course / Practicum / Industrial Project Track 的理由，以及 12 周实习或 6 units 两学期企业项目的规划。
- 呼应官网「identify, formulate, and apply their learned knowledge in solving problems」与「advanced knowledge in technologies such as cyber security, blockchain, data science, AI ... while maintaining a broad understanding of finance and law」，突出技术 + 金融 + 监管的复合定位，而非纯 CS 或纯金融。
- 呼应官网选修方向（FTEC5550 Elements of RegTech for the Financial Market、FTEC5640 Decentralized Finance、FTEC5660 Agentic AI for Business and FinTech），结合香港 / 大湾区 FinTech 生态说明具体职业目标。
