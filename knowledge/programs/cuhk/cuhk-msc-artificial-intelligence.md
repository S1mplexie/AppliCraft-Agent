## 课程结构

**结构说明**：27 credits。Required courses 10 units（另一官网页面写 9 units）、Core Elective Courses 6 units（至少修 6 units）、Elective Courses 11 units（含 Project；另一页面写 12 units，至少修 12 units）——分组学分官网不一致，待核实。Elective 分为 Theoretical Group、Applications Group、Data Analytics Group、Practicum and Projects Group。官网注明 Individual courses and the programme curriculum are subject to review and revision without prior notice.

**核心课程 / 必修**

- AIMS5701 Fundamentals in Artificial Intelligence
- AIMS5702 Artificial Intelligence in Practice
- AIMS5703 Emerging Technologies in Artificial Intelligence
- AIMS5704 Machine Learning Theory

**选修课程 / 方向**

- AIMS5710 Deep Learning Fundamentals and Theories
- AIMS5720 Computer Vision and Pattern Recognition
- AIMS5730 Natural Language Processing
- AIMS5740 Generative Artificial Intelligence
- AIMS5760 Technology Commercialization of AI Innovations
- AIMS5761 From Business Plan to Investable AI Ventures
- AIMS5780 Artificial Intelligence Internship
- AIMS5790 Artificial Intelligence Project I
- AIMS5791 Artificial Intelligence Project II
- ELEG5757 Intelligent Wearable Electronics

官网未分设 track

## 项目特色与就业

**项目特色**

- 官网：Offered by the Faculty of Engineering, the Master of Science in Artificial Intelligence (MSc AI) Programme aims to train graduates with comprehensive state-of-the-art knowledge and relevant expertise in AI.
- 官网：students will not only have solid foundation knowledge in AI, but also have accumulated experience in practical applications and project development, enabling them to apply AI technologies in real life.
- 官网（CUHK 教务会 2024-25 第一次会议，2024-10-16）：批准 self-financed Master of Science Programme in Artificial Intelligence，生效 2025-26 → 属新开项目，2025/26 首招，非改名 / 停办 / 合并；同批批准 MSc in Robotics。
- 官网：30+ elective courses available for building a flexible curriculum tailored to students' career-development objectives；涵盖 robotics、life science and medicine、financial technology、Internet-of-Things and edge AI、computational imaging、neuromorphic computing、speech and language processing、IC design、big-data analytics。
- 官网：Faculty of Engineering hosts 30 IEEE Fellows and 4 ACM Fellows；AI / machine learning 研究归于 Information and Automation Technology，为 CUHK 四大 Strategic Areas 之一。
- 官网：Students can opt for the course titled 'From Business Plan to Investable AI Ventures'；Internship 为至少 12 weeks（学术导师 + 企业导师双指导）；6-unit 2-semester project course；Mentorship and Industrial Interactions。
