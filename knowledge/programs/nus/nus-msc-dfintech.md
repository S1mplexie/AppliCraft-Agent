## 课程结构

**结构说明**：官网明确『The MSc DFinTech is a master's degree by coursework programme』：7 门 core（28 Units，其中从 11 门 FT50xx 中任选 3 门 12 Units + 4 门必修 16 Units）+ 3 门 elective（12 Units）+ 12 Units capstone（FT5007 或 3 门选修替代），共 52 Units；官方 Annex A 标注『Applicable to August 2026 Intake onwards』

**核心课程 / 必修**

- FT5001 Fintech Innovations: A Strategic Landscape
- FT5005 Machine Learning for Finance
- FT5011 Deep Learning for Finance
- BMD5301 Introduction to Finance for FinTech Professionals
- BMD5302 Financial Modelling for FinTech Professionals
- IT5001X Software Development Fundamentals
- IT5006 Fundamentals of Data Analytics

**选修课程 / 方向**

- FT5003 Blockchain Innovations
- FT5004 Programming for Blockchain Applications
- FT5009 Contemporary Topics in Financial Data Analytics
- FT5010 Algorithmic Trading Systems Design and Deployment
- CS5224 Cloud Computing
- IS5008 Technology Risk & Cyber Resilience

**Track / 专攻方向**

- Computing Technologies
- Financial Data Analytics and Intelligence
- Digital Financial Transactions and Risk Management

## 项目特色与就业

**项目特色**

- 官网：『this graduate programme in FinTech is designed to help students build a strong foundation in computing and finance』，选修按三条 tracks 组织
- 官网 Programme Vision Statement：『To be the world's leading Master of Science in Digital Finance and AI, developing future leaders with entrepreneurial and inclusive mindsets, supported by a powerful alumni network and industry partners ... anchoring Singapore as a trusted financial hub for the world』
- 官网：设 Programme Industry Advisory Committee（成员来自 Monetary Authority of Singapore、DBS、Ant International、Trust Bank Singapore、Stripe、Circle、Warwick Business School 等）
- 官网：Capstone『Students can choose either an academic research project under the supervision of world-class scholars at NUS, or a FinTech internship』；毕业最低 GPA 3.0

**就业去向（仅官网明确表述）**

- 官网：『careers as AI software developers, data scientists, FinTech security specialists, financial quantitative analysts and other similar professions』
- 官网：『in financial institutions or FinTech firms』
- 官网 Programme Industry Advisory Committee 成员来自 Monetary Authority of Singapore、DBS、Ant International、Trust Bank Singapore、Stripe、Circle 等（为顾问委员会构成，非就业去向数据）
- 具体就业率 / 薪资 待核实

## 文书参考要点（PS / SOP）

- 呼应官网『build a strong foundation in computing and finance』与三条 tracks（Computing Technologies / Financial Data Analytics and Intelligence / Digital Financial Transactions and Risk Management）：文书须说明 computing 与 finance 双背景的交汇点及目标 track。
- 官网『non-computing students must provide sufficient evidence of python programming knowledge and/or evidence of learning advanced quantitative subjects』：非计算机背景申请者必须具体举证 Python 能力与高等量化课程。
- 官网职业表述『careers as AI software developers, data scientists, FinTech security specialists, financial quantitative analysts ... in financial institutions or FinTech firms』：未来职业规划段落应对齐这些岗位与机构类型。
- Personal Statement 官网原文『reasons for applying to the programme, your preparation for the field of study, future career plans』；并注意官网『Work experiences in FinTech, AI, or Data Analytics will be advantageous, but are not required』——有相关经历则突出，无则不必虚构。

> 以上要点依据项目官网原文提炼，供撰写 why-this-programme / 职业规划段落时参考；不得直接照抄为个人经历，也不得把官网表述当作已核实的项目事实写入硬指标。
