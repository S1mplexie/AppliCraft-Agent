## 课程结构

**结构说明**：官网：『Students will complete 5 essential courses to build a cross-disciplinary foundation for Business Analytics』；『students are required to take three elective courses from at most two of the following vertical sectors』；The Professional Consulting Capstone Course 为年期内课程，含 capstone classes、industry analytics seminars 与 3-6 个月全职 capstone project；毕业需最终 CAP 3.0

**核心课程 / 必修**

- Analytics in Managerial Economics
- Data Management and Warehousing
- Operations Research and Analytics
- Foundations of Business Analytics
- Advanced Analytics and Machine Learning

**选修课程 / 方向**

- Big Data Analytics Techniques
- Consumer Data Analytics
- Financial & Risk Analytics
- Healthcare Analytics
- Statistical Modelling

**Track / 专攻方向**

- Big Data Analytics Techniques
- Consumer Data Analytics
- Financial & Risk Analytics
- Healthcare Analytics
- Statistical Modelling

## 文书参考要点（PS / SOP）

- 必答指定文书题（官网原文）『What is your point of view about the AI and Business Analytics trends in the industry of your interest? Describe what you have done, and what you plan to do in short-term and mid-term to ride on this trend.』：≤350 词，须含行业观点、已做之事、短期与中期计划三部分。
- 官网『Answer in a written essay in English of no more than 350 words, a three-minute video, or both. A video is highly preferred』：文书系统应同时产出 ≤350 词文字稿与 3 分钟视频脚本，并控制字数上限。
- 官网『Declaration on the use of AI tools - Complete the declaration honestly』：以 AI 辅助生成文书时须如实申报，知识库须保留原创性与 AI 使用声明提示。
- 官网『Be a Catalyst of Digital Transformation』与『co-designed by NUS Business School and NUS Computing』：文书宜体现技术 + 商业双视角，避免纯技术叙事；并呼应『Strong Mathematics foundation』用量化经历举证。
