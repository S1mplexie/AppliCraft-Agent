## 课程结构

**结构说明**：官网明确『The MComp (General Track) programme is a Master by coursework programme』：6 essential + 4 elective + 1 capstone，共 52 Units；官方 Annex A（with effect from AY2024/2025, Semester 1，July 2026 版）规定 IT5001 与 IT5003 必读、另从 6 门中任选 4 门；capstone 可选 CP5105 或 CP5106 + 2 门 Industry Readiness Courses

**核心课程 / 必修**

- IT5001 Software Development Fundamentals
- IT5003 Data Structures and Algorithms
- IT5004 Enterprise Systems Architecture Fundamentals
- IT5005 Artificial Intelligence
- IT5006 Fundamentals of Data Analytics
- CP5105 Computing Capstone Project

**选修课程 / 方向**

- IT5002 Computer Systems and Applications
- IT5007 Software Engineering on Application Architecture
- IT5008 Database Design and Programming
- CS5228 Knowledge Discovery and Data Mining
- CS5321 Network Security
- CS5224 Cloud Computing

**Track / 专攻方向**

- Computing Systems
- Cybersecurity
- Data Analytics
- Enterprise IT
- Financial Technology (FinTech)
- Robotics
- Software Methodology

## 项目特色与就业

**项目特色**

- 官网：『The MComp (General Track) programme is designed primarily for students with non-computing undergraduate degrees and aims to provide a systematic pathway for graduates who wish to embark on an accelerated conversion to the computing field』
- 官网：『prepare MComp graduates for future career challenges in the Infocomm sector』
- 官网：capstone『A two-semester capstone project is aimed at allowing students to pick up practical software engineering and system design skills』，『includes an option with a computing internship component』；可选『a four-month industry internship from mid-May to mid-September』
- 官网：『Students who have completed the School of Computing Graduate Certificate in Computing Foundations I and/or II may apply to use the set of foundation courses completed to meet up to 24 Units of the essential course requirements』；毕业最低 GPA 3.0

## 文书参考要点（PS / SOP）

- 呼应官网『designed primarily for students with non-computing undergraduate degrees』与『a systematic pathway for graduates who wish to embark on an accelerated conversion to the computing field』：转码动机与系统性路径是文书主线。
- 官网『preference will be given to applicants with IT related job experiences』：用编程自学、项目或 IT 实习实证，弥补非计算机本科背景。
- 官网『includes an option with a computing internship component』及『a four-month industry internship from mid-May to mid-September』：未来职业规划段落可对应这一实践路径。
- Personal Statement 官网原文『reasons for applying to the programme, your preparation for the field of study, future career plans』：按四要素铺陈，避免只写动机不写先修准备。

> 以上要点依据项目官网原文提炼，供撰写 why-this-programme / 职业规划段落时参考；不得直接照抄为个人经历，也不得把官网表述当作已核实的项目事实写入硬指标。
