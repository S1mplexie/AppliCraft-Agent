## 课程结构

**结构说明**：40 Units；三个 specialisation（Computer Science / Information Systems / Infocomm Security）。下列 core 为官方 Annex A 所列 CS specialisation 课程池（非固定必修），官网原文『The details of the pool of courses available are listed in Annex A - MComp (CS SPN)』；Annex 标注『with effect from August 2026 Intake』

**核心课程 / 必修**

- CS5228 Knowledge Discovery and Data Mining
- CS5223 Distributed Systems
- CS5234 Algorithms at Scale
- CS5321 Network Security
- CS5340 Uncertainty Modeling in AI
- CS5446 AI Planning and Decision Making

**选修课程 / 方向**

- level 4000 to 6000 courses offered by the School of Computing（最多 2 门 level 4000 课程）
- CS4249 Phenomena and Theories of Human-Computer Interaction
- CS5218 Principles and Practice of Program Analysis
- CS5229 Advanced Computer Networks
- CS5232 Formal Specification and Design Techniques
- CS5250 Advanced Operating Systems

**Track / 专攻方向**

- Computer Science
- Information Systems
- Infocomm Security

## 项目特色与就业

**项目特色**

- 官网：『a comprehensive and challenging graduate programme with area specialisations. It encompasses latest research findings, both applied and fundamental. The programme also provides advanced and in-depth knowledge of IT to prepare the students for challenges in IT career』
- 官网 MComp (General Track) 页称 MComp 为『a flagship graduate programme from the National University of Singapore, School of Computing』
- 官网：『The programme may be taken full-time or part-time. Most level 5000 courses are conducted in the evenings.』
- 官网：毕业需『achieve a minimum final GPA of 3.0』；完成 Graduate Certificate 者可申请最多 12 Units 学分转移

**就业去向（仅官网明确表述）**

- 待核实（官网未提供系统性就业岗位 / 雇主 / 就业率数据）
- 官网表述：『prepare the students for challenges in IT career』（IT 职业）
- 官网表述：『future career challenges in the Infocomm sector』（信息通信行业）
- 官网校友案例：Guo Ai, Software Engineer, Goldman Sachs, Awardee of the IMDA Excellence in Computing Prize 2025

## 文书参考要点（PS / SOP）

- 呼应官网 Personal Statement 原文『describe succinctly your reasons for applying to the programme, your preparation for the field of study, future career plans, and other aspects of your background and interests that may help in the evaluation of your aptitude and motivation for graduate studies』，按申请动机—专业准备—未来职业规划—其他背景四段式组织。
- 官网『preference will be given to students with IT related job experiences』：文书须用具体 IT 实习／项目而非泛泛兴趣来论证 readiness for graduate study。
- 官网四条学历路径中三条含『2 years IT industry experience』：本科非 computing 荣誉学位者，须在文书中说明 IT 行业经历如何补足学术背景。
- 官网『Candidates with degrees in Electronic Commerce/ Engineering/ Economics/ Statistics/ Mathematics can also be considered for admission to the Information Systems specialisation』：跨量化／商科背景申请 IS 方向时可据此论证专业适配度。

> 以上要点依据项目官网原文提炼，供撰写 why-this-programme / 职业规划段落时参考；不得直接照抄为个人经历，也不得把官网表述当作已核实的项目事实写入硬指标。
