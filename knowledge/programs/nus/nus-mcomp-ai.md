## 课程结构

**结构说明**：40 Units；官方 Annex A（Applies to August 2025 Intake onwards）把 essential 课程分为 4 个 sub-areas：Perception / Reasoning and Planning / Learning and Optimization / AI Governance；Coursework Option 需从至少 3 个 sub-area 选 5 门 essential

**核心课程 / 必修**

- CS4243 Computer Vision and Pattern Recognition
- CS6207 Advanced Natural Language Processing
- CS4263 Artificial Intelligence Principles
- CS5446 Reinforcement Learning and Sequential Decision Making
- CS5242 Neural Networks and Deep Learning
- CS5562 Trustworthy Machine Learning
- IS5010 AI Ethics and Governance

**选修课程 / 方向**

- level 4000 to 6000 courses offered by the School of Computing（最多 2 门 level 4000）
- CS5246 Text Mining
- CS5477 3D Computer Vision
- CS5339 Theory and Algorithms for Machine Learning
- CS5340 Uncertainty Modelling in AI
- IS5152 Data-Driven Decision Making

**Track / 专攻方向**

- Perception
- Reasoning and Planning
- Learning and Optimization
- AI Governance

## 文书参考要点（PS / SOP）

- 呼应官网『designed for students with solid foundation in computing and mathematics』与『preference will be given to applicants with strong mathematical training and related project or work experience』，用具体数学课程与 ML/AI 项目实证，而非空泛兴趣。
- 官网『systematic breadth-and-depth training in advanced principles, algorithms, and applications in AI』配合四个 sub-areas（Perception / Reasoning and Planning / Learning and Optimization / AI Governance）：文书宜指明目标 sub-area 并对应到具体课程。
- 官网 Annex A『Students who have not done an undergraduate AI course are advised to do CS4263 concurrently or before doing other courses in Sub-Area 2』：缺 AI 先修者可主动写出补修计划，体现 preparation for the field of study。
- Personal Statement 官网原文四要素『reasons for applying to the programme / preparation for the field of study / future career plans / aptitude and motivation for graduate studies』；职业目标可呼应『careers in industry as well as research』。
