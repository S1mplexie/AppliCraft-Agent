## 课程结构

**结构说明**：31 学分 = 核心 13 学分 + 选修 18 学分；MSDM 6771 Data-Driven Modeling Seminars and Tutorials 为全体必修 1 学分、为期一年、两个常规学期均需参加；经 Program Director 批准可修读 MSc(MAFM) 最多 3 学分选修；兼读制每学期最多 9 学分

**核心课程 / 必修**

- MSDM 5001 Introduction to Computational and Modeling Tools
- MSDM 5002 Scientific Programming and Visualization
- MSDM 5003 Stochastic Processes and Applications
- MSDM 5004 Numerical Methods and Modeling in Science
- MSDM 6771 Data-Driven Modeling Seminars and Tutorials

**选修课程 / 方向**

- MSDM 5005 Innovation in Practice
- MSDM 5051 Programming Algorithm and Architecture with AI
- MSDM 5053 Quantitative Analysis of Time Series
- MSDM 5054 Statistical Machine Learning
- MSDM 5055 Deep Learning for Modeling: Concepts, Tools, and Techniques

**Track / 专攻方向**

官网未分设 track

## 项目特色与就业

**项目特色**

- 2027/28 目录 Introduction：「Jointly offered by the Departments of Physics and Mathematics」「aims to cultivate the next generation to have strong skills in problem solving and logical thinking so that they become competent in the data science sector」。
- 2027/28 目录 Introduction：「By bridging theory, practice and techniques ... hands-on experience in analyzing large amounts of data ... facilitating smart decision making for businesses, industries and services」。
- MSDM 6771 课程描述：「expose students to a variety of issues in data science and industry, and to enhance students' communication with industry experts and faculty」；为期一年，全体必修。
- 物理系官方文章 physics.hkust.edu.hk/zh-hans/node/3526：「The curriculum and program structure seamlessly integrate industry instructors and feature seminar speakers from diverse sectors」；2025 年迎来第 6 届（6th cohort）毕业；首 5 年课程评审获高度正面评价。
- 项目官网：面向「some science or engineering background」学生，培养「modeling skills based on information extracted from data」及 problem solving、logical thinking 能力。

**就业去向（仅官网明确表述）**

- 物理系官方文章（前 5 届就业分布）：Engineering >24%；Data science / Algorithm 与 AI-related 21.5%；banking / finance 20.4%；Education / Government 6.8%；Entrepreneurship 2.0%；继续深造 14.7%。
- 物理系官方文章：典型职位为 software engineers、data scientists、machine learning engineers；雇主含 Tencent、Bank of China、Ant Group、P&G、L'Oréal。
- 项目官网 Career & Prospect：提供一系列 career-focused workshops and seminars；非本地应届毕业生可依 Immigration Arrangements for Non-local Graduates (IANG) 申请留港工作。

## 文书参考要点（PS / SOP）

- 呼应官网「training students with some science or engineering background ... modeling skills based on information extracted from data」，文书需讲清理工背景如何转向数据建模，而非只写对数据科学的兴趣。
- 呼应「strong skills in problem solving and logical thinking」与 Learning Outcomes「Handle large amount of data using computational tools」，用具体计算 / 建模项目支撑能力叙事。
- 呼应 Additional Information「Have working knowledge in at least one computer language, and basic training in calculus and linear algebra」，逐条自证编程语言、微积分与线性代数基础。
- 呼应物理系官方文章「seamlessly integrate industry instructors and feature seminar speakers from diverse sectors」及校友建议「set their goals early. Which job sector are they targeting?」，文书宜给出明确目标行业与实习 / 项目选择逻辑。
