## 课程结构

**结构说明**：30 学分 = Required Courses 16 学分 + Elective Courses 14 学分；选修课自 School of Business and Management 课程组合中选取，具体名单于每届入学初公布；经 Academic Director 批准可多修最多 4 学分选修

**核心课程 / 必修**

- ISOM 5270 Big Data Analytics
- ISOM 5460 Project Management
- ISOM 5510 Data Analysis
- ISOM 5535 High Dimensional Statistics with Business Applications
- ISOM 5610 Introduction to Business Analytics
- ISOM 5620 Visual Analytics for Business Decisions
- ISOM 5715 Decision Analytics
- ISOM 5810 Business Modeling and Optimization

## 项目特色与就业

**项目特色**

- 官网首页：「The MSBA Program is designed to equip students with the skills to effectively leverage information and business analytics knowledge in building data-driven strategies while effectively communicating business value」。
- 官网首页 Program Characteristics：Specialized Consulting Track and AI-Ready Curriculum；Advisory Board of Industry Leaders；Extensive Industry and Alumni Network；Comprehensive Career Resources；Drive Innovation and Create Value；World-Class Faculty with Academic Excellence and Industry Expertise。
- Academic Director 致辞：「Our primary focus is not merely on the execution stage of analyzing business data but on developing skillsets for value creation with the ability to communicate across various business stakeholders」。
- Academic Director 致辞：「equip students to act as interfaces between data analytic technical teams and business counterparts」；并列出三类目标人群（recent graduates and business professionals / technically skilled graduates and engineers / experienced professionals in 2-year Part-Time program）。
- Academic Director 致辞：课程含「innovative discussion-based courses」「mandatory corporate-style training workshops」「corporate consulting track ... real consulting projects with participating companies」。
- 2027/28 目录 Learning Outcomes：「Play leading roles in successful business strategy execution using analytics」。

**就业去向（仅官网明确表述）**

- Academic Director 致辞所列校友雇主：McKinsey、KPMG、Goldman Sachs、PwC、Citigroup、Tencent、Alibaba、AIA。
- Academic Director 致辞所列目标职位类型：data analytics translator、cyber-security consultant、expert in people analytics、product manager，或将既有会计 / 金融服务技能与高级数据分析能力结合。
- 官网 Program Characteristics 列 Comprehensive Career Resources 与 Extensive Industry and Alumni Network；Advisory Board 由资深高管及校友组成，支持 curriculum enhancement。

## 文书参考要点（PS / SOP）

- 呼应官网「Our primary focus is not merely on the execution stage of analyzing business data but on developing skillsets for value creation with the ability to communicate across various business stakeholders」，文书不能只写技术，要写价值创造与跨部门沟通。
- 呼应「equip students to act as interfaces between data analytic technical teams and business counterparts」，突出「技术—业务桥梁」定位，并用具体协作经历佐证。
- 呼应「prospective applicants should already possess basic skills in statistics and relevant programming languages such as SQL and Python」，需逐项自证统计与编程先修技能，因官网强调课程 intensive。
- 呼应「corporate consulting track ... real consulting projects with participating companies」与面试要求「familiarize themselves with the program design and vision as outlined on our website」，文书须紧扣项目 vision 并说明为何需要真实企业咨询训练。

> 以上要点依据项目官网原文提炼，供撰写 why-this-programme / 职业规划段落时参考；不得直接照抄为个人经历，也不得把官网表述当作已核实的项目事实写入硬指标。
