## 课程结构

**结构说明**：至少 30 学分 CSIT 课程；Required Courses 栏仅写「Students are required to complete at least 30 credits of CSIT courses」并列出 CSIT 6910 Independent Project；经 Program Director 批准可修读 MSc(BDT) 的 MSBD 课程最多 3 学分；最多可转移 9 学分；兼读制每学期最多 9 学分；毕业需 GGA 2.850 或以上

**核心课程 / 必修**

- CSIT 6910 Independent Project（1-3 学分，可重复至最多 6 学分）

**选修课程 / 方向**

- CSIT 5100 Engineering Reliable Software Systems
- CSIT 5710 Cryptography
- CSIT 5730 Principles of Cybersecurity
- CSIT 5740 Introduction to Software Security
- CSIT 5750 Introduction to Network Security
- CSIT 5760 Blockchain and Smart Contracts
- CSIT 6000 Topics in Information Technology

**Track / 专攻方向**

- Cybersecurity Concentration（须修至少 9 学分网络安全课程 + 3 学分 CSIT 6910 Independent Project）

## 项目特色与就业

**项目特色**

- SENG 项目页 Overview：「designed for those who wish to obtain broad-based and leading-edge skills in Information Technology and provides students with a strong theoretical foundation in all areas of IT」。
- SENG 项目页 Overview：「The dual emphasis on a strong theoretical foundation and practical application, combined with extensive skill-set training ... enabling them to meet both the present and future needs of the IT industry」。
- 2027/28 目录 Introduction：「For those who have interests to focus in cybersecurity, they could opt for cybersecurity concentration available in the program」。
- 2027/28 目录 Learning Outcomes：「Apply the latest computing and software development tools and techniques, especially state-of-the-art techniques that are used in industrial practice」。
- 官网说明课程通常安排在工作日晚间以及周六上午或下午于 HKUST 校园内授课；可跨选 MSc(BDT) 最多 3 学分 MSBD 课程。

**就业去向（仅官网明确表述）**

- SENG 项目页 Career Prospect 职位：Program Analysts、Business Analysts、Cloud Architects、Cyber Security Analysts、Data Analysts、Data Engineers、Database Administrators、IT consultants、IT Specialists、Information Systems Managers、Machine Learning Engineers、System Engineers。
- SENG 项目页：多数毕业生在原籍国就业，部分留港；部分升读 HKUST 或海外大学的 MPhil / PhD 项目。

## 文书参考要点（PS / SOP）

- 呼应官网「The dual emphasis on a strong theoretical foundation and practical application, combined with extensive skill-set training」，文书需同时给出理论功底与工程实践的证据，而非只罗列掌握的工具。
- 呼应「For those who have interests to focus in cybersecurity, they could opt for cybersecurity concentration」，可明确写出选择 Cybersecurity Concentration 及对应课程（如 CSIT 5730 Principles of Cybersecurity）的动机。
- 呼应 Learning Outcomes「Apply the latest computing and software development tools and techniques, especially state-of-the-art techniques that are used in industrial practice」，用具体工业级项目说明跟进主流技术的能力。
- 呼应「Subject to the approval of the Program Director, students may take a maximum of 3 credits of MSBD courses from the MSc Program in Big Data Technology」，可写明跨选 MSc(BDT) 课程的计划，体现跨项目学习视野。
