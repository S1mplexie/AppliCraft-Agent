## 课程结构

**结构说明**：36 学分 = Required Courses 至少 27 学分 + Elective Courses 最多 9 学分；选修可为数学系 4000 级以上课程、系外 5000 级以上课程及 / 或 MAFS 6100；兼读制每学期最多 9 学分

**核心课程 / 必修**

- MAFS 5010 Stochastic Calculus
- MAFS 5020 Advanced Probability and Statistics
- MAFS 5030 Quantitative Modeling of Derivatives Securities
- MAFS 5040 Quantitative Methods for Fixed-Income Instruments
- MAFS 5130 Quantitative Analysis of Financial Time Series
- MAFS 5140 Statistical Methods in Quantitative Finance
- MAFS 5210 Mathematical Models of Investment
- MAFS 5220 Quantitative Risk Management
- MAFS 5240 Software Development with C++ for Quantitative Finance
- MAFS 5270 Mathematical Market Microstructure
- MAFS 5280 Financial Markets in Hong Kong and China
- MAFS 5310 Portfolio Optimization: Theory and Application
- MAFS 5340 Machine Learning and Its Applications
- MAFS 5360 Computing for Finance in Python

**选修课程 / 方向**

- 待核实：2027/28 目录 Elective Courses 仅写「Up to 9 credits；The elective courses can be any mathematics courses at 4000-level or above, or any courses outside the department at 5000-level or above, and/or MAFS 6100」，未逐门列出选修课名

**Track / 专攻方向**

官网未分设 track

## 项目特色与就业

**项目特色**

- 2027/28 目录 Introduction：「Financial mathematics is an amalgam of mathematics, statistics, finance theory and computer science」。
- 2027/28 目录 Introduction：「The MSc program is continuously enhanced with new courses in machine learning and Blockchain technology, among others, to connect to emerging trends in quantitative finance」。
- 2027/28 目录 Learning Outcomes：「Design and evaluate quantitative models for derivatives pricing, portfolio management and trading strategies」「Formulate appropriate risk monitoring procedures ... perform effective scenario simulation using statistical techniques in risk assessment」「Devise computer systems for analysis of financial data」。
- 项目页 Admission Information：「looking for candidates with a solid background in undergraduate-level mathematics, statistics, finance, and computing」。
- 历史改名：2010/11 目录名为「MSc in Mathematics (Financial Mathematics and Statistics)」，2013/14 起为「MSc Program in Financial Mathematics」；2027/28 名称稳定，无停办或合并。

**就业去向（仅官网明确表述）**

- 项目官网 Placement Statistics（旧届别数据，对应 Cohort 2014/15、2015/16、2018/19，非 2026/27 或 2027/28）：2018/19 届平均首年薪资 HKD 342,976、中位数 233,472、最高 840,000。
- Job function（2018/19 届）：Risk Management 22.37%、Trading Support & Strategy 21.05%、Quantitative Analyst 21.05%、Investment Banking 7.89%、System Developer 7.89%、Management Trainee 6.58%、Auditing & Model Validation 3.95%、Investment Management 2.63%。
- Job location（2018/19 届）：Mainland (China) 55.38%、Hong Kong SAR 43.08%、Others 1.54%。
- 项目官网岗位描述：analyst、trading assistant、consultant，分布于 banking、consultancy、investment banking、asset management、insurance、information technology；部分加入 financial technology firms 任 system developers。
- 项目官网雇主名单：Agricultural Bank of China、Bank of Communications、China CITIC Bank、China Construction Bank、Citi group、HSBC、Hang Seng Bank、ICBC、Nomura、J.P. Morgan、Societe Generale、Standard Chartered Bank、UBS、DBS、Deloitte、Ernst & Young、KPMG、PricewaterhouseCoopers、China Galaxy Securities、CICC、China Securities (International)、Huatai Financial Holding、HKEX、Magnum Research Limited、Manulife、ASTRI、Numerix。
