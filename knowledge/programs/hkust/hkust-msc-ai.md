## 课程结构

**结构说明**：30 学分 = 核心 12 学分 + 选修 18 学分；选修中 Specialized AI Technology Courses 至少 6 学分、Application-oriented AI Courses 至少 3 学分；ARIN 6900 Independent Project 为 6 学分 Practicum Training；最多可转移 9 学分；经 Program Director 批准可于第一学期开始 Independent Project；兼读制每学期最多 9 学分；毕业需 GGA 2.850 或以上

**核心课程 / 必修**

- ARIN 5101 Advanced Python Programming for Artificial Intelligence
- ARIN 5102 AI Fundamentals: Concepts and Methods
- ARIN 5103 Foundations of Machine Learning
- ARIN 5104 Artificial Intelligence Ethics

**选修课程 / 方向**

- ARIN 5201 Machine Learning for Computer Vision
- ARIN 5202 Machine Learning for Natural Language Processing
- ARIN 5203 Foundation Models and Generative Artificial Intelligence
- ARIN 5204 Reinforcement Learning
- ARIN 5301 Human-Computer Interaction
- ARIN 5302 Medical Image Analysis
- ARIN 5303 Artificial Intelligence in Cybersecurity
- ARIN 5304 Artificial Intelligence in Healthcare
- ARIN 5305 Artificial Intelligence in Software Engineering
- ARIN 6900 Independent Project
- ARIN 6000 Special Topics

**Track / 专攻方向**

- Specialized AI Technology Courses（选修类别，至少 6 学分）
- Application-oriented AI Courses（选修类别，至少 3 学分）
- Practicum Training：ARIN 6900 Independent Project

## 项目特色与就业

**项目特色**

- 2027/28 目录 Introduction：「AI has evolved beyond being solely an area of computer science」「addresses the latest trends in AI development and aims to meet the growing demand for skilled AI practitioners」「tailored to both recent graduates and working professionals」。
- SENG 项目页 Overview：「design, deploy, evaluate, and manage AI systems while upholding ethical and legal standards」；「Through courses and practicum training, students will gain a comprehensive understanding of the fundamental principles and concepts of AI」。
- 近年新开：2024/25 课程目录返回 Page Not Found；2025/26 目录首次出现（学费 HK$360,000）；HKUST 官方活动日历 2024-09-27 说明称「HKUST's new Master of Science (MSc) program in Artificial Intelligence」「Offered by the Department of Computer Science and Engineering」。首届入学年份官网无明文，依据目录推断为 2025/26 → 待核实。
- 核心课设 ARIN 5104 Artificial Intelligence Ethics，2026/27 目录 Learning Outcomes 含「Analyze ethical and social implications of AI technologies ... bias, fairness, privacy, and accountability」。
- 课程通常安排在工作日晚间以及周六上午或下午于 HKUST 校园内授课（SENG 项目页）。

**就业去向（仅官网明确表述）**

- SENG 项目页 Career Prospect：「a wide range of career opportunities in various sectors beyond traditional Computer Science and Engineering roles」。
- SENG 项目页 Career Prospect：「not only in large companies but also in small startups established in response to government initiatives」。
- SENG 项目页 Career Prospect：「offering graduates the flexibility to thrive in the dynamic AI landscape both locally and globally」。

## 文书参考要点（PS / SOP）

- 呼应官网「AI has evolved beyond being solely an area of computer science」，文书应呈现 AI 在某一具体应用领域的落地（如医疗、网络安全、软件工程），而非只写算法课程。
- 呼应「design, deploy, evaluate, and manage AI systems while upholding ethical and legal standards」与核心课 ARIN 5104 Artificial Intelligence Ethics，需体现对 bias、fairness、privacy、accountability 的认识。
- 呼应「tailored to both recent graduates and working professionals」，应届生与在职申请者应分别突出学习潜力或行业痛点，避免套用同一套叙事。
- 呼应入学要求「A bachelor's degree in other disciplines with at least two years of post-qualification work experience relevant to the program」，跨专业申请者须明确写出两年相关工作经历及其与 AI 的关联。

> 以上要点依据项目官网原文提炼，供撰写 why-this-programme / 职业规划段落时参考；不得直接照抄为个人经历，也不得把官网表述当作已核实的项目事实写入硬指标。
