## 课程结构

**结构说明**：30 学分 = 核心课程 12 学分 + 选修课程 18 学分；经 Program Director 批准可修读 MSc(IT) 的 CSIT 课程最多 6 学分；最多可转移 9 学分；兼读制每学期最多 9 学分；毕业需 GGA 2.850 或以上

**核心课程 / 必修**

- MSBD 5001 Foundations of Data Analytics
- MSBD 5002 Data Mining and Knowledge Discovery
- MSBD 5003 Big Data Computing
- MSBD 5004 Mathematical Methods for Data Analysis
- MSBD 6910 Independent Project（MicroMasters 证书持有者豁免 4 门核心课后替换）

**选修课程 / 方向**

- MSBD 5005 Data Visualization
- MSBD 5006 Quantitative Analysis of Financial Time Series
- MSBD 5007 Optimization and Matrix Computation
- MSBD 5008 Introduction to Social Computing
- MSBD 5009 Parallel Programming
- MSBD 5010 Image Processing and Analysis

**Track / 专攻方向**

官网未分设 track

## 项目特色与就业

**项目特色**

- 官网 2027/28 目录 Introduction：「Jointly offered by the Departments of Computer Science and Engineering and Mathematics」「integrates different disciplines together to allow students to know all the important aspects of the big data」。
- SENG 项目页 Distinct Features：「First of its kind in Hong Kong and has been a leader among many similar programs in the region」。
- SENG 项目页 Distinct Features：「Well balance between the foundations of the subjects and popular topics with state-of-the-art knowledge」。
- SENG 项目页 Distinct Features：「Hands-on independent project with industry partners engaging students with practical applications for future career or PhD study」。
- 2027/28 目录 Learning Outcomes：「Identify, explain, and use Big Data infrastructure」「Derive knowledge and strategies from Big Data analytics and apply them to privacy protection and policy making」「Investigate existing problems on Big Data and conduct original Big Data research」。
- 课程覆盖面（SENG 项目页）：infrastructure、data integration、storage、modeling and management、computing systems、analytic and mining systems、security、policy and social implications、human factors、big data applications (data science)。

**就业去向（仅官网明确表述）**

- SENG 项目页 Career Prospect 职位：Data Analyst、Data Engineer、Database Administrator、Data Scientist、Machine Learning Engineer、Statistician、Business Analyst。
- SENG 项目页 Career Prospect 行业：e-commerce、education、finance、healthcare、media and entertainment、retail、travel、telecom。
- SENG 项目页：多数毕业生在原籍国的金融或科技公司就业，部分留港；部分继续在 HKUST 或其他大学攻读 PhD。

## 文书参考要点（PS / SOP）

- 呼应官网「Jointly offered by the Departments of Computer Science and Engineering and Mathematics」与「integrates different disciplines」，文书应同时呈现计算机与数学两条能力主线，而非只写编程或工具使用。
- 呼应 Learning Outcomes「Derive knowledge and strategies from Big Data analytics and apply them to privacy protection and policy making」，可用具体项目说明对数据治理、隐私保护与政策影响的理解。
- 呼应「Hands-on independent project with industry partners ... for future career or PhD study」，需明确毕业去向是就业还是读博，并让科研/实习经历与该路径一致。
- 呼应入学要求「Applicants with a bachelor's degree in other disciplines must have relevant working experience in IT and Mathematics related fields」，跨专业申请者须量化说明该 IT / 数学相关工作经验。

> 以上要点依据项目官网原文提炼，供撰写 why-this-programme / 职业规划段落时参考；不得直接照抄为个人经历，也不得把官网表述当作已核实的项目事实写入硬指标。
