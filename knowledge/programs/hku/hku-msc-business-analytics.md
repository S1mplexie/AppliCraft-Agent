## 课程结构

**结构说明**：Students need to complete a total of ten courses； 官网课程页为 4 Core + 5 Elective + 1 Capstone 并附 Boot Camp；可选 Artificial Intelligence (AI) stream 或 Management Consulting (MC) stream，最多 2 门选修可取自 School 其他授课型硕士课程

**核心课程 / 必修**

- Python for Data Analytics
- Business Statistics
- Decision Analytics
- Operations Analytics
- Business Analytics Capstone（Capstone 选项之一）
- Business Case Analysis Capstone（Capstone 选项之一）

**选修课程 / 方向**

- Machine Learning
- Deep Learning
- Forecasting and Predictive Analytics
- Applied Large Language Models
- Generative Artificial Intelligence
- Big Data Analytics on the Cloud
- Quantitative Trading
- Ethics in Artificial Intelligence
- Business Simulation
- Geospatial and Business Analytics
- Storytelling with Data
- Financial Engineering

**Track / 专攻方向**

- Artificial Intelligence (AI) stream
- Management Consulting (MC) stream

## 项目特色与就业

**项目特色**

- 「offers a comprehensive curriculum that aims to help students develop both hard and soft skills related to business analytics throughout the programme」
- 「Our students will learn the knowledge and skills of understanding data, transforming data into information and converting information into executable actions」
- 「There is currently an unprecedented industry demand for business analytics professionals」
- 「The programme boasts a real world business analytics project - The Capstone Project - with reputable corporations in the region」，学生把所学用于 project sponsors 的 real life business problem
- 「a rigorous learning experience across a wide spectrum of fields, including big data techniques, business statistics, simulation and forecasting models, operations and supply chain management, and financial and marketing analytics」，工具含 R 与 Python

**就业去向（仅官网明确表述）**

- 待核实（官网 未列具体岗位清单； 有「unprecedented industry demand for business analytics professionals」， 站点提及 Career Development Team 提供 employer connections、workshops、coaching 等支持）

## 文书参考要点（PS / SOP）

- 呼应官网「understanding data, transforming data into information and converting information into executable actions」，PS 可按「数据 → 信息 → 可执行决策」三段组织动机与经历。
- 官网「develop both hard and soft skills related to business analytics」表明除技术外重软技能， 亦提及沟通、critical thinking、teamwork、leadership，宜用团队项目或客户沟通经历佐证。
- 官网明言 Boot Camp 服务「Those without a pure quantitative background can build up essential competencies」，跨专业申请者可据此说明如何补齐数学、编程、数据库与统计基础。
- 官网「The Capstone Project - with reputable corporations in the region … to a real life business problem facing our project sponsors」与 AI / MC 两个 stream要求 PS 明确方向选择并写出实践导向。
