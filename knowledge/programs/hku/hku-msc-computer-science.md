## 课程结构

**结构说明**：72 credits：Courses 60（Discipline Courses 至少 48，含对应 stream 至少 24；Elective Courses 最多 12）+ Capstone Course 12；官网未设统一必修核心课清单，学生按所选 stream 修读，core 列的是官网课程表中跨 stream 的代表性 Discipline Courses 与 Capstone

**核心课程 / 必修**

- COMP7404 Computational intelligence and machine learning
- COMP7103 Data mining
- COMP7408 Distributed ledger and blockchain technology
- COMP7906 Introduction to cyber security
- DASC7606 Deep learning
- COMP7705 Project（Capstone）
- COMP7706 Computing technology practicum（Capstone）

**选修课程 / 方向**

- COMP7105 Advanced topics in data science
- COMP7106 Big data management
- COMP7107 Management of complex data types
- COMP7108 Network data analytics
- COMP7305 Cluster and cloud computing
- COMP7608 Large language models
- COMP7412 Banking in Web3.0 – Metaverse, DeFi, NFTs and beyond
- FITE7414 Generative AI in financial services
- COMP7503 Multimedia technologies
- COMP7507 Visualization and visual analytics

**Track / 专攻方向**

- General Stream
- Cyber Security
- Financial Computing
- Multimedia Computing
- AI & Decentralized Technologies (AI x Web3)

## 文书参考要点（PS / SOP）

- 呼应官网「General Stream Offers students the most flexibility to design their own study portfolio based on their career aspiration or requirements」，且 要求申请时即选定 pathway/stream，PS 必须写明目标 stream 及其与职业目标的对应关系。
- AIDT 方向官网定位「Designed for future innovators and technology leaders」，强调 technical expertise、industry exposure 与 global perspective，适合写 AI / Web3 与产业结合的志向。
- 呼应官网「equips students with advanced knowledge and practical expertise in emerging technologies」与「students can explore a diverse range of cutting-edge fields, from data engineering, cloud computing, blockchain, and artificial intelligence to financial fraud analytics」，用具体技术经历对应官网列出的领域。
- Cyber Security 官方描述从「technical, organisational, human, and legal perspectives」切入、Financial Computing 侧重「data, algorithms, and practical numerical methods」，PS 可据此展示对目标方向能力结构的理解。

> 以上要点依据项目官网原文提炼，供撰写 why-this-programme / 职业规划段落时参考；不得直接照抄为个人经历，也不得把官网表述当作已核实的项目事实写入硬指标。
