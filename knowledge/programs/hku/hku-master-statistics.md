## 课程结构

**结构说明**：60 credits：Two compulsory courses 12 credits + Theme-specific elective courses 24 credits + 其他选修与 Capstone（ 2027-28 课程页）；可选 general approach，或专攻 Risk Management / Data Analytics / Financial Statistics 主题并印于成绩单

**核心课程 / 必修**

- STAT7101 Fundamentals of statistical inference
- STAT7102 Advanced statistical modelling

**选修课程 / 方向**

- STAT7008 Programming for data science
- STAT8017 Data mining techniques
- STAT8021 Big data analytics
- STAT6013 Financial data analysis
- STAT8003 Time series forecasting
- STAT8020 Quantitative strategies and algorithmic trading
- STAT8308 Blockchain data analytics
- STAT6015 Advanced quantitative risk management
- STAT8002 Project（Capstone 选项）
- STAT8088 Practicum（Capstone 选项）
- STAT8089 Capstone project（Capstone 选项）

**Track / 专攻方向**

- Risk Management
- Data Analytics
- Financial Statistics

## 项目特色与就业

**项目特色**

- 「rigorous training in the principles and the practice of statistics」并「emphasizes in applications」，通过 computer-aided and hands-on experience 为 further study、research、consulting work 与 administration 做准备
- 「The programme offers great flexibilities for students who wish to take a general approach, or a specialized theme in Risk Management, Data Analytics, or Financial Statistics」
- Programme Highlights：「Be a knowledgeable statistician in principles and practice」「Experience hands-on applications of methodologies with powerful statistical software」「Could select electives from the School's research postgraduate courses」「Join the programme of more than 30 years in curriculum development and delivery」
- 官网「extensive analysis of structured or unstructured data」，涵盖 spatial data analysis、advanced statistical inference、big data analytics、marketing analytics、data mining techniques 及 SAS、R、Python 等统计软件（ 官网版本）
- 「The programme emphasises applications and aims to train knowledgeable statisticians, preparing candidates for consulting work or research in the field」

**就业去向（仅官网明确表述）**

- Actuary
- Algorithm Engineer
- Data Analyst
- Data Engineer
- Equity Data Specialist
- Investment Portfolio Manager
- Quantitative Trader
- Risk Management Analyst
- Risk Assurance Associate
- Statistical Officer

## 文书参考要点（PS / SOP）

- 呼应官网「Students joining the programme are expected to come from a wide range of disciplines」与其列出的 science、social sciences、engineering、medical sciences、information systems、business and finance 本科背景，跨专业申请者应说明原专业如何为统计方法提供领域知识。
- 官网「emphasizes in applications」「through computer-aided and hands-on experience」与「hands-on applications of methodologies with powerful statistical software」表明重实操，PS 宜用 SAS / R / Python、数据分析项目或实证经历佐证。
- 官网「great flexibilities … general approach, or a specialized theme in Risk Management, Data Analytics, or Financial Statistics」要求 PS 明确主题方向或 general 取向，并对应 相应 theme 的选修课。
- 官网区分两类人群「recommended for those at work to be part-time students … as well as for fresh graduates to be full-time students」，应届生与在职者应分别论证 1 年全日制 / 2 年兼读制的学制选择。
