## 课程结构

**结构说明**：须完成 Project 与 10 门课程，共 75 至 81 credits：Discipline Courses 不少于 51 credits（含 3 门 List-A Disciplinary compulsory courses）、Elective Courses 最多 2 门 / 12 credits、Capstone Experience（Project）12 credits；List-B 至少 3 门（List-B-1 与 List-B-2 各至少 1 门），List-C 至少 2 门（List-C-1 与 List-C-2 各至少 1 门）

**核心课程 / 必修**

- FITE7409 Blockchain and cryptocurrency（非 computer science 背景者修读）
- COMP7408 Distributed ledger and blockchain technology（computer science 背景者修读）
- MFIN7002 Investment analysis and portfolio management
- LLAW6093 Regulation of financial markets
- FITE7001 Project（12 credits Capstone）

**选修课程 / 方向**

- COMP7802 Introduction to financial computing
- FITE7407 Securities transaction banking
- COMP7906 Introduction to cyber security
- FITE7410 Financial fraud analytics
- ECOM7126 Machine learning for business and e-commerce
- FITE7405 Techniques in computational finance
- COMP7412 Banking in Web3.0 – Metaverse, DeFi, NFTs and beyond
- FITE7414 Generative AI in financial services
- DASC7606 Deep learning
- COMP7404 Computational intelligence and machine learning
- STAT8020 Quantitative strategies and algorithmic trading
- LLAW6046 Privacy and data protection

**Track / 专攻方向**

官网未分设 track

## 项目特色与就业

**项目特色**

- 「The HKU Master of Science in Financial Technology and Data Analytics [MSc(FTDA)] Programme aims to prepare students for a career in the ever-changing world of FinTech」
- 「The programme consists of foundation courses in computing, statistics, finance, and law」，跨学科覆盖计算、统计、金融与法律，含 Faculty of Law 的 9-credit 课程

## 文书参考要点（PS / SOP）

- 呼应官网「a Bachelor's degree in Engineering or Science discipline」，PS 开头需先论证本科专业归属；非 CS 背景者官网还规定必修选 FITE7409 而非 COMP7408，可顺势说明自己的技术起点。
- 官网「foundation courses in computing, statistics, finance, and law」与必修「LLAW6093 Regulation of financial markets」显示项目重合规/监管维度，对 RegTech、金融监管有兴趣是差异化切入点。
- 呼应「aims to prepare students for a career in the ever-changing world of FinTech」，适合写行业快速变化与技能持续更新的动机，并对应 的 Generative AI in financial services、Banking in Web3.0 等课程。
