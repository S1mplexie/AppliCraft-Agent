## 课程结构

**结构说明**：72 credits：Compulsory Courses 24 + Disciplinary Electives 36（List A 至少 12、List B 至少 12）+ Capstone Requirement 12

**核心课程 / 必修**

- COMP7404 Computational intelligence and machine learning
- DASC7011 Statistical inference for data science
- DASC7104 Advanced database systems
- STAT7102 Advanced statistical modelling（招生系统规章 写法；官网课程页 写作 DASC7102 Advanced statistical modelling for data science，见 verification.conflicts）
- DASC7600 Data science project（12 credits Capstone）
- DASC8088 Data science practicum（6 credits Capstone 选项）

**选修课程 / 方向**

- DASC7606 Deep learning
- COMP7107 Management of complex data types
- COMP7305 Cluster and cloud computing
- STAT7008 Programming for data science
- STAT8308 Blockchain data analytics
- STAT8307 Natural language processing and text analytics
- COMP7409 Machine learning in trading and finance
- STAT8017 Data mining techniques

**Track / 专攻方向**

官网未分设 track

## 项目特色与就业

**项目特色**

- 「The programme's interdisciplinarity promotes the applications of computer technology, operational research, statistical modelling, and simulation for problem-solving and decision-making in organisations and enterprises within the private and public sectors」
- 「The curriculum of the MDASC programme adopts a well-balanced and comprehensive pedagogy of both statistical and computational concepts and methodologies」
- 「Solid foundation in statistical and computational analyses」与「Hands-on applications of methodologies with software」
- 「Capstone project with real-life applications」及「Electives cover a broad range of contemporary topics about Computer Science and Statistics」
- 「The curriculum is designed to provide graduates with training in the principles and practice of data science」

**就业去向（仅官网明确表述）**

- AI Engineer
- Algorithm Engineer
- Data Analyst
- Data Scientist
- Machine Learning Engineer
- Senior Data Science & AI Research Assistant
- Senior Technical Analyst
- Software Engineer (Machine Learning)

## 文书参考要点（PS / SOP）

- 呼应官网「Computer Science / IT + Statistics + Domain/Business Knowledge + Machine Learning + Software Development + Research」的跨学科定位，用具体项目说明自己如何打通统计与计算两条线，而非只强调单一技能。
- 官网称项目「ideal for those interested in acquiring analytical skills in areas ranging from statistics to computational analytics」，并明确列出 science、engineering、medical sciences、social sciences、information systems、computing and data analytics 等本科背景；跨专业申请者可据此论证原专业与数据科学的契合点。
- 呼应官网「Solid foundation in statistical and computational analyses」与「predictive and prescriptive concepts and methodologies」，PS 宜逐项对应展示 calculus and algebra、computer programming、introductory statistics 三门先修课与量化、编程能力。
- 官网强调「training in the principles and practice of data science」与「Capstone project with real-life applications」，适合写「理论训练 + 真实问题落地」双线动机。
