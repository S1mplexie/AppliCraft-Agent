## 课程结构

**结构说明**：72 credits：Compulsory Courses 42 + Disciplinary Electives 18 + Capstone Project 12；官网注明若选修课程与既往学习内容相似，Department 可不予批准

**核心课程 / 必修**

- ARIN7001 Foundations of artificial intelligence
- ARIN7011 Optimization in artificial intelligence
- ARIN7013 Numerical methods in artificial intelligence
- ARIN7101 Statistics in artificial intelligence
- ARIN7102 Applied data mining and text analytics
- COMP7404 Computational intelligence and machine learning

**选修课程 / 方向**

- ARIN7015 Topics in artificial intelligence and machine learning
- MATH7224 Topics in advanced probability theory
- MATH7502 Topics in applied discrete mathematics
- MATH7503 Topics in advanced optimization
- STAT6011 Computational statistics and Bayesian learning
- STAT7008 Programming for data science
- STAT8020 Quantitative strategies and algorithmic trading

**Track / 专攻方向**

官网未分设 track

## 项目特色与就业

**项目特色**

- 「an interdisciplinary taught postgraduate programme jointly offered by the Department of Mathematics (host) and the School of Computing and Data Science」
- 「Its academic focus is promoting the applications of mathematics, statistics, and computer science to facilitate AI in decision-making and problem-solving for various organizations and enterprises within the private and public sectors」
- 「Interdisciplinary and well-balanced curriculum」：core courses 提供 solid training，electives 覆盖 mathematics、statistics、computer science，并有 capstone project with real-life applications、guest lectures by distinguished scholars and industry experts、internship opportunities in the AI industry and academia
- 「Learning within and beyond AI」与「The theoretical elements in the curriculum will help students develop essential intellectual capacity at large」
- 「Teaching team has strong connections with high-tech industries in the Greater Bay Area」

**就业去向（仅官网明确表述）**

- software engineers
- consultants
- research scientists in AI and related fields such as big data and financial technology

## 文书参考要点（PS / SOP）

- 呼应官网「an interdisciplinary taught postgraduate programme」与「applications of mathematics, statistics, and computer science to facilitate AI in decision-making and problem-solving」，按数学、统计、计算机三条线逐项对应先修课「linear algebra, calculus, probability theory, introductory statistics, and computer programming」，证明量化与编程准备度。
- 官网强调面向「organizations and enterprises within the private and public sectors」的 decision-making / problem-solving，适合写「用 AI 解决真实组织决策问题」的动机，而非仅表达技术兴趣。
- 呼应「capstone project with real-life applications」与「internship opportunities in the AI industry and academia」，可结合课程项目、科研或实习经历展开落地能力。
- 官网「The theoretical elements in the curriculum will help students develop essential intellectual capacity at large」表明项目重理论训练，PS 宜体现愿意接受扎实数学、优化与统计基础的取向。
