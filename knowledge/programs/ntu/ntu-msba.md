## 课程结构

**结构说明**：核心课 + 选修课，设三个 specialised concentrations：Finance / Marketing / Technology & Operations；采用 hands-on 方式，含两个 projects 把课堂知识用于真实商业情境；课程与行业伙伴共同设计，可接触 real data 与 practicum / internship；官网注明 The programme undergoes continuous improvement. As such, modules might be subject to changes；总学分数待核实。

**核心课程 / 必修**

- AI and Big Data in Business
- Advanced Database Management
- Advanced Programming
- Analytics and Machine Learning in Business
- Analytics Strategy
- Applications of Statistics
- Data Management and Visualisation
- Design Thinking & Technology Management
- Programming Essentials
- Strategies for Digital Transformation in Business

**选修课程 / 方向**

- Time Series for Business Analytics
- AI with Advanced Predictive Techniques in Finance
- Data Analytics for Credit and Related Risk
- Information Systems & ERP (SAP)
- Causal Inference
- Digital Marketing in the Age of AI
- Advanced Prescriptive Analytics with Generative AI
- Introduction to Cyber Security
- Leading Business Analytics Projects
- Machine Learning Methodology
- Office Process Automation with Python
- Robotic Process Automation (RPA) for Analytics

**Track / 专攻方向**

- Finance
- Marketing
- Technology & Operations

## 项目特色与就业

**项目特色**

- designed to bridge the gap between business strategy and data technology（官网原文）
- By leveraging data, you will redefine customer experiences, guide organisations through digital disruptions and drive profitable digital transformations（官网原文）
- Your journey begins with future-ready modules like Analytics and Machine Learning in Business, followed by the opportunity to build deep expertise in your chosen field through three specialised concentrations（官网原文）
- The learning for this programme adopts a very hands-on approach, with two projects allowing participants to apply classroom knowledge to real-world business situations（官网原文）
- Our curriculum is designed alongside industry partners to meet evolving business demands. You will gain access to real data and internship opportunities through a practicum（官网原文）
- Leverage the combined prestige and technical expertise of NTU's College of Computing and Data Science and the School of Physical and Mathematical Sciences（官网原文；学位仍由 NBS 提供）

**就业去向（仅官网明确表述）**

- 官网：Graduates from the programme are typically considered in business analyst roles
- 官网点名实习/雇主：internship opportunities with industry leaders such as DBS, KPMG and GE Digital
- 官网 programme-overview：You will be able to lead analytics projects, both as the head of a business unit and analytics business lead, or as a professional consultant with business domain expertise
