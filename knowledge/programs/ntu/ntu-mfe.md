## 课程结构

**结构说明**：20 core courses + 4 elective courses；课程分三组：Financial, Mathematics & Statistics / Finance / Computing；3 trimesters（全日制 1 年）或 6 trimesters（兼读制 2 年），每 trimester 分 2 个 7 周 mini-term；可选 CMU Track（最后 7 周、4 门课，完成获 Certificate in Computational Finance by CMU）；官网注明 Elective courses are changed from time to time、Courses offered in CMU are subject to changes；课程在 NTU 主校区、工作日晚间及部分周六授课；总 AU 数待核实。

**核心课程 / 必修**

- Calculus & Linear Algebra
- Stochastic Modeling in Asset Pricing
- Stochastic Calculus for Finance
- Probability & Statistics
- Linear Financial Models
- Financial Time Series Analysis
- Corporate Finance
- Asset Pricing Theory
- Bond Portfolio Management
- Equity Portfolio Management
- Derivative Securities
- Interest Rate Derivatives
- Financial Risk Management
- Object Oriented Programming I
- Object Oriented Programming II

**选修课程 / 方向**

- Numerical Methods for Financial Instrument Pricing (E)
- Simulation Methods for Option Pricing (CMU)
- Advanced Statistical Modeling (E)
- Optimization in Finance (E)
- Advanced Risk Management (CMU)
- Exotic Options & Structured Products (E)
- Credit Risk - Measurement & Management (E)
- Programming Web Applications in Finance (E)
- Artificial Intelligence Techniques in Finance (E)
- Financial Computing (CMU)
- Financial Engineering Project (CMU)

**Track / 专攻方向**

- CMU Track
- Non-CMU Track

## 项目特色与就业

**项目特色**

- With over 20 years of history, MSc Financial Engineering is one of the most established programmes in the region（官网原文）
- A unique graduate programme, the Master of Science in Financial Engineering (MFE) empowers you to succeed as a dynamic professional in the world of high technology finance（官网原文）
- equip you with concepts and techniques in Finance, Computer Science and Mathematics；apply sophisticated engineering tools to financial problems（官网原文）
- Have the option to spend a seven-week term studying at Carnegie Mellon University (CMU), USA（官网原文）；合作自 1999 年项目创立起，完成 4 门课获 Certificate in Computational Finance by CMU
- Industry experts from the Speaker Series, exclusively organised for our MFE participants（官网原文）
- NTU-SJTU Double Master's Programme: Graduate with 2 degrees in 24 months（官网原文）

**就业去向（仅官网明确表述）**

- 官网：Our MSc Financial Engineering graduates work primarily in the financial services industry
- 官网 programme-overview 列岗位方向：risk management、quantitative asset management、product structuring、quantitative trading、quantitative research、financial information technology 等 high-technology finance 领域
- 官网点名招聘机构：DBS (Development Bank of Singapore)、Standard Chartered、JPMorgan Chase & Co.、Goldman Sachs
