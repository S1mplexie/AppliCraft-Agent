## 课程结构

**结构说明**：30 AUs；除 Capstone Project 外每门课 3 AUs、39 教学小时、13 周；两学期 Capstone Project 可通过 industry internship 或 applied cybersecurity project 完成；官网 Curriculum 标签为 View the Course Content Here 链接、并注明 the curriculum is subject to change；精确课程代码与 core/elective 划分待核实。CCDS 学院页称核心课由本院教师讲授，部分实用选修课由 industry experts 讲授。

**核心课程 / 必修**

- Computer Security（官网 Why This Programme? 模块领域用词，非课程代码）
- Application and Software Security（官网模块领域用词，非课程代码）
- Cryptography（官网模块领域用词；CCDS 页称 core technical modules such as computer and network security and cryptography）
- Network Security（官网模块领域用词，非课程代码）

**选修课程 / 方向**

- Security Monitoring and Threat Detection（官网模块领域用词；CCDS 页另作 security monitoring and operations）
- Malware Analysis（官网模块领域用词）
- Digital Forensics（官网模块领域用词）
- Mobile App and Cloud Security and Zero Trust Architecture（官网模块领域用词）
- AI and Cybersecurity（官网模块领域用词；主项目页另称 New modules such as AI in Cybersecurity）
- Introduction to AI and Cyber Threat Intelligence（官网模块领域用词）

**Track / 专攻方向**

官网未分设 track

## 项目特色与就业

**项目特色**

- 官网标语 Secure today's systems. Protect tomorrow's digital world.
- Cybersecurity is no longer confined to IT departments. It sits at the heart of business resilience, national security and digital innovation（CCDS 官方页原文）
- Our new Master of Science in Cybersecurity (Applied Cybersecurity) builds on our established MSc Cybersecurity programme (launched in 2022-2023)（CCDS 官方页原文，说明改名/刷新而非停办）
- This new program is designed for both midcareer professionals and also new university graduates（官网原文）；部分模块 Co-developed with experienced cybersecurity practitioners，由 industry experts 授课
- students may also undertake an industrial attachment with companies；The programme culminates in a two-semester Capstone Project（官网主项目页原文）
- Cybersecurity Meets AI / Built for an AI-Enabled Threat Landscape（官网原文）；官网列出 AI-driven threat detection、Threat intelligence、Adversarial machine learning、AI system security、AI supply chain risks 等方向
- #4 in the world for Data Science and Artificial Intelligence, according to the 2026 QS World University Rankings by Subject（官网原文）

**就业去向（仅官网明确表述）**

- 官网主项目页 Top 5 Careers in Cybersecurity：Security Consultant / Information Security Analyst / Security Architect / Chief Information Security Officer / Security Engineer
- CCDS 官方页 Career Opportunities：Cybersecurity Consultant、Information Security Analyst、Security Engineer、Security Architect、Digital Forensics Specialist、Threat Intelligence Analyst、Security Operations Lead
- 官网称毕业生 ready to take on advanced roles as cybersecurity professionals, analysts, and leaders

## 文书参考要点（PS / SOP）

- 依据官网「designed for both midcareer professionals and also new university graduates」，在职与应届申请人应分别明确自身职业阶段与转型/入门动机。
- 依据官网「It sits at the heart of business resilience, national security and digital innovation」，文书可把技术兴趣上升到业务连续性与社会安全层面。
- 依据官网「Built for an AI-Enabled Threat Landscape」及 AI-driven threat detection / adversarial machine learning / AI system security 等表述，AI 与安全交叉是最适合作为「为何选此项目」论点的官网特色。
- 呼应官网申请门户必填项「reason for pursuing the programme」与「selection is competitive」，说明两学期 Capstone Project 或 industrial attachment 与自身职业目标的关联。

> 以上要点依据项目官网原文提炼，供撰写 why-this-programme / 职业规划段落时参考；不得直接照抄为个人经历，也不得把官网表述当作已核实的项目事实写入硬指标。
