## 项目特色与就业

**项目特色**

- The MSc in Information Systems (MSIS) programme blends theory and practice to equip students with cutting-edge knowledge necessary for the design, development, maintenance, and management of information systems to provide better user experience（官网原文）
- Broad-based perspective of information systems education from experienced, multi-disciplinary faculty and industrial practitioners（官网原文）
- Offers a strong emphasis in users and management through theory and practice-based experiential learning（官网原文）
- Provides a solid foundation for mid-career professionals wanting to establish themselves in the industry（官网原文）
- 官网 Curriculum 四大领域：Software Development and Infrastructure / Human Computer Interaction / Management of Systems and Services / Data Analytics and Artificial Intelligence
- MSIS students select them based on their individual learning goals and career objectives（官网 Curriculum 原文）；官网另设 MSIS Scholarship，标榜 Kickstart your career in technology and data!

**就业去向（仅官网明确表述）**

- 待核实（官网项目页无 Top careers / Career Prospects 列表；已查首页、Curriculum、Additional Information 标签及官方 Admission Guide。仅 IN6228 课程描述提及 better prepared to take on management or consulting roles in IT，奖学金页有 Kickstart your career in technology and data! 宣传语）

## 文书参考要点（PS / SOP）

- 依据官网「Submission of personal statement and referee letters are not compulsory, however, applicants may submit them if it is a value-add to their application」，PS 非强制但应主动作为加分项提交。
- 依据官网「MSIS students select them based on their individual learning goals and career objectives」，文书宜明确个人学习目标与职业目标，并对应到四大课程领域中的具体选课逻辑。
- 依据官网「it is recommended for applicants to have at least a minimum of one-year of full-time working experience in relevant fields」且「Internships are not counted towards working experience」，需正面说明相关全职经历，勿以实习替代。
- 依据官网「Provides a solid foundation for mid-career professionals wanting to establish themselves in the industry」与「a strong emphasis in users and management」，中期转型者应突出用户视角与管理维度的实践。

> 以上要点依据项目官网原文提炼，供撰写 why-this-programme / 职业规划段落时参考；不得直接照抄为个人经历，也不得把官网表述当作已核实的项目事实写入硬指标。
