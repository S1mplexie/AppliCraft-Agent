## 课程结构

**结构说明**：30 AUs：12 AUs 核心课 + 18 AUs（AI Master Project 与选修课）；每门课 3 AUs、39 contact hours（lectures / tutorials / example classes / labs）、13 周；AI Master Project 为一年 6 AUs，官网明确 The master project is not a compulsory component；bridging course Python Programming 0 AU、非必修。官网 Curriculum 标签仅提供课程 PDF 链接（coursecontent_msai_13mar25.pdf），本次仅能提取 Programme Overview 文字，精确课程代码/正式课名待核实。

## 项目特色与就业

**项目特色**

- coursework-based graduate degree designed for professionals and graduates who want to deepen their expertise in AI theory, methodologies, and applications（官网原文）
- trains students to design, implement, and manage AI systems while also cultivating the skills to address project management, policy, and ethical considerations（官网原文）
- Hands-on learning is embedded throughout the programme via course projects, an AI Master Project, and internship opportunities（官网原文）
- leading innovations in AI deployment, building intelligent systems, and shaping the responsible use of AI technologies in Singapore and globally（官网原文）
- #4 in the world for Data Science and Artificial Intelligence, according to the 2026 QS World University Rankings by Subject（官网原文）
- FlexiMasters in Artificial Intelligence Fundamentals 经 NTU PACE 提供，合资格 Singaporeans / PRs 可获 SkillsFuture Singapore (SSG) 课程费共同资助（官网原文）

**就业去向（仅官网明确表述）**

- 官网 Top 5 Careers in Artificial Intelligence：Machine Learning Engineer / Data Scientist / Business Intelligence Developer / Research Scientist / Big Data Engineer/Architect
- 官网称毕业生 will be prepared to take on advanced roles in industry, government, and research

## 文书参考要点（PS / SOP）

- 呼应官网申请门户必填项「reason for pursuing the programme」，PS 应正面回答选择该项目的动机，并与 AI theory / methodologies / applications 三层定位对齐。
- 依据官网「designed for professionals and graduates who want to deepen their expertise in AI theory, methodologies, and applications」，在职与应届申请人应分别突出实践深度或研究潜力。
- 依据官网「cultivating the skills to address project management, policy, and ethical considerations」与「shaping the responsible use of AI technologies」，文书可加入 AI 治理、伦理与落地管理的具体经历或志向。
- 依据官网「Meeting these criteria does not guarantee admission, as selection is competitive」，须以文书体现超出最低录取门槛的竞争力；GRE 与推荐信为 Optional，可作为额外佐证。

> 以上要点依据项目官网原文提炼，供撰写 why-this-programme / 职业规划段落时参考；不得直接照抄为个人经历，也不得把官网表述当作已核实的项目事实写入硬指标。
